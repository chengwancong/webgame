原始提示词：

> Create ONE original playable game character sprite: a charming anthropomorphic Shiba Inu adventurer inspired only by the familiar Doge Shiba features (red-gold fur, cream muzzle/cheeks, triangular ears, slightly sideways amused glance), NOT a photograph and no coin, logo, text or meme lettering. TRUE low-resolution pixel-art design, like an expertly drawn 48-pixel-tall RPG character, displayed as an exact nearest-neighbor enlarged sprite. One visible pixel grid throughout, no subpixel shading or soft edges. Single character standing, three-quarter view facing RIGHT, full body, large expressive Shiba head (about half the character height), very short compact body, small brown boots and paws. Deep navy blue short adventurer jacket with a cream collar and small brass belt buckle; small curled red-gold tail visible behind the left hip. Both ears readable; cream eyebrows; black small shiny nose, two clean dark eyes with a gently mischievous side glance, cheerful confident expression, compact muzzle. A small straight steel short sword in the character's RIGHT paw, held outward and DOWN to the right at rest, clearly separated from torso and legs. Keep arms and two legs distinct and readily separable for skeletal rigging, no cape, no hat, no shield or backpack. Clean bold silhouette with a selective one-pixel dark warm/navy outline, 3 shades per material, broad intentional pixel clusters, light upper left, limited approximately 24-color palette with no pure black; no dithering, gradients, anti-aliasing, blur, particles, environment, ground, contact shadow, frame, scenery, labels or other characters. Center the character on a truly transparent background with generous transparent padding. IMPORTANT intended native frame is 64x64 pixels with character silhouette 48px high; make the design simple enough to reproduce faithfully at that size. This is the exact design to be split into head, torso, two arms, two legs, tail and sword, not an illustration.
