# r2 结构色面参考

模式：内置 image_gen；透明背景。生成结果只作结构/配色参考，不当作帧图或动作交付。

输入：evidence/r2-native-A10e.png、evidence/r2-native-A7e.png。

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Produce ONE production color-and-anatomy reference sheet containing these two existing game dragons side by side, no text. Reference 1 is upright A10 dragon; Reference 2 is low finned A7 dragon. Keep the exact respective silhouette, pose, proportions, muzzle length, jaw, crown/horns, claws, layered fin/wing membranes and tail; do not redesign them as worms or round beans, no additional legs, no enlarging wings. Repaint inside the existing anatomy in refined cute adventure-game flat hard-edged hand-painted color planes, thin warm brown #3c2b23 outlines. 4–5 distinct hard value planes, not gradients or realistic texture. A10: deep brick-red dorsal scales with cream/gold segmented belly, darker wing bone and copper membranes. A7: dark teal dorsal body with pale gold segmented belly and distinctly warm copper/orange fin membranes, thin structural fin ribs. Preserve original scale plate locations, brow ridge as a neutral anatomical plane, distinct long snout and closed mouth corner. Remove visible teeth/fangs. Almond-shaped eye with large colored round iris and round pupil, light sclera and highlights: NOT dots, NOT vertical slits or angry eyebrows. No armor, collar, scarf, gemstones or magic effect in this reference. This sheet is only a painting reference; final deliverables will be original Spine animations, so preserve native anatomy faithfully.

输出：evidence/r2-imagegen-anatomy-reference.png。最终零件使用可编辑矢量层落实原生分区与新五官，在原画布、alpha和UV约束中回填；未直接用整张生成图替换动画。
